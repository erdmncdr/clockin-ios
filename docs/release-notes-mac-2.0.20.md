# Clockin 2.0.20

- The Chrome extension now talks to Clockin directly. Each press shows "Clockin ✓ Clock in" or "Clockin ✓ Clock out" on the page once Clockin has really started or stopped, and the extension's icon shows a check mark. Chrome no longer asks to open Clockin.
- Only your own presses count: switching tabs, pages that click their buttons by themselves, or another "Clock out" elsewhere on the page never start or stop the timer.
- After updating: in chrome://extensions remove the old Clockin extension, then in Clockin press Settings > General > Prepare the extension again, load it again and pick your buttons once more.
- If Clockin's icon does not show in the menu bar, Today now offers Reset position, Menu Bar settings and Copy details. Settings > Menu bar has Reset menu bar position and Copy menu bar details too.
