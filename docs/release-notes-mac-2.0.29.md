# Clockin 2.0.29

- One timer counts once. When the same session was ended on both your Mac and your iPhone (the second device had not yet seen the first clock-out), both entries used to stay and the hours and earnings doubled. Now the first clock-out is the entry; the later one goes to Settings > iCloud > Sync changes, where you can still see it.
- The same rule applies to the first merge preview when you connect a new device.
