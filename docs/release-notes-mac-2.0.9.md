# Clockin 2.0.9

- The font is now a setting of its own: System, Rounded, Serif or Monospaced, in Settings > General next to Theme. Themes change colors only, and everyone starts on the System font. Each option shows in its own design.
- History is simpler: the By day / Sessions switch and the plus button are gone. Entries are always grouped by day; add a past entry with the plus on Today.
- Fixed labels with rolling digits cutting off their last letter, such as "kaldı" in the money momentum card.
