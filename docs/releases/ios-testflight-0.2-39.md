# iOS 0.2 (39): A storm for the level-up

Repository: `erdmncdr/clockin-ios`.
Source: main at `c5cb66de98d8241b71906a877df4f3e706dce20a`.

- The level-up sound is now a storm, built to sound fuller and less
  synthetic. Wind rises and gusts through the charge with thunder rolling
  far off and a low drone swelling under it, every arc cracks as an
  electric zap and a heartbeat lands on every pulse. The strike is a
  thunderclap over a sub boom and struck metal, then an overdriven wall of
  low fifths holds while the screen is bright and fades with the light. A
  new rank gets a smaller thunderclap and the wall a fifth higher.
- Sustained voices drift and breathe, the hall is a convolution reverb and
  the mix is glued with compression and saturation.
- Everything from 0.2 (38).
