# iOS 0.2 (30): Rank and medal identities

Repository: `erdmncdr/clockin-ios`.
Source: main at `892b1a6b05ec15e1f7e69131828b7a3079aaab1f`.

- Each of the nine level ranks has its own plate shape, metal, stone cut and
  motion, following the [design brief](../rank-and-medal-design.md). The moon
  rides the Orbit ring, a corona turns behind Solar, Nova fires a shock ring,
  Celestial draws constellations and Eternal gets wings.
- Mission medals have a reveal and a continuous effect per tier, and their
  emblems move by family.
- Motion pauses for Reduce Motion, Low Power Mode, an inactive scene and
  badges that are off screen. Still frames still show each signature.
- Everything from 0.2 (29).
