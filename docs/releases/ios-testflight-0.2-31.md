# iOS 0.2 (31): MMO level-up and a pass over every screen

Repository: `erdmncdr/clockin-ios`.
Source: main at `c76220df286c4c7a1f88801eed6112e7c7cfc64d`.

- The level-up is a full-screen moment in three beats, with a forged crest,
  a column of light, each rank's own light, the companion as a space warrior
  and a haptic pattern on the same timeline. A new rank gets a second beat.
  See [the design](../level-up-design.md).
- The Eternal badge's iridescence flows without restarting.
- Desk mode keeps the room clear of the timer.
- A pass over every screen, all eight themes, a small screen and the largest
  text sizes tidied labels, layouts, number formats and titles; the entry
  editor can delete its entry; the small widget shows the companion.
- Everything from 0.2 (30).
