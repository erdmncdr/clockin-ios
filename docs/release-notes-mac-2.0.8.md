# Clockin 2.0.8

- A new floating tab bar in translucent glass, like on iPhone: Today, History and Progress. Settings opens from the gear on Today, Settings… (Command-comma) and the menu bar.
- Settings is now a page of its own with categories on the left: General, Timer & goals, Pay & currency, Menu bar, Notifications, iCloud, Data & import, and Help & about.
- Native Mac buttons, menus and segmented controls throughout. Pause and Clock out are full-width again, with Clock out in red.
- History and Progress have proper margins and read as one page.
- On the History chart, swipe left or right with two fingers on the trackpad to move to the previous or next period; the Left and Right arrow keys do the same. Hover over a bar to see its value. Right-click an entry to edit or delete it. Future days can no longer be selected.
- Desk mode fills the whole screen, with the timer above your companion and a close button in the corner.
- The pinned timer no longer cuts off the TRY amount when an older version had saved a very narrow window.
