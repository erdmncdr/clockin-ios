# Clockin 2.0.6

- A new menu bar icon: Clockin's helmet without the antenna, as large as the other menu bar icons.
- Today, History, Progress and Settings are now tabs at the bottom of the window, as on iPhone. Command-1 to Command-4 still switch between them.
- Settings > Menu bar > Show details in the menu bar: turn it off to show only the Clockin icon, for example when your iPhone's Live Activity already shows the timer in the menu bar.
- The pinned focus chime and focus radio cards on Today have proper switches and menus.
- Importing timecards is safer: if your entries change while you review an import, Clockin asks you to review again instead of deleting anything you did not see; if some rows of a file cannot be read, it tells you how many and deletes nothing; pasted pages with Submitted or Draft rows can still clean up their period.
- Syncing no longer closes the first merge preview while you read it, and recovers on its own after a failed fetch.
- The re-import message counts each doubled entry once and shows the real extra hours.
