# Clockin 2.0.24

- The Chrome extension is now called Pulse. Its bar while picking buttons and its "✓ Clock in" notice on the page show Pulse instead of Clockin.
- The extension's icon fills its space in Chrome's toolbar like other extensions' icons, instead of looking smaller.
- After updating: in chrome://extensions press the reload button on the extension (it then shows as Pulse). Your picked buttons stay.
