# Clockin 2.0.21

- The Chrome extension recognises your Clock out button even when the site moves it, for example when a notice appears above it or the Clock in button disappears. Before, a moved button could be missed or taken for the other one.
- While you pick your buttons, the Clockin hint is a small bar in a bottom corner. It moves to the other corner if a button sits under it, so it never covers Clock in.
- If a press on something labelled Clock in or Clock out does not count, the extension's popup says so and suggests picking the buttons again.
- After updating: in chrome://extensions press the reload button on Clockin. Picking your buttons once more is recommended, so the extension remembers more about them.
